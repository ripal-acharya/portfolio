Drone controlled by hand gestures and face tracking

Capstone program at Turku University of Applied Sciences, January to April 2026. My role: human-robot interaction developer.

Won the university capstone competition for innovation.

What I built

  • Real-time recognition of 10+ hand gestures with MediaPipe and OpenCV.
  • Face tracking, so the drone can follow a person on its own.
  • Keyboard control that can take over at any time.
  • Smart switching between gesture, face and keyboard modes, so the drone moves smoothly without flickering.
  • Live feedback on screen, such as the gesture name and battery level.

Built with Python, MediaPipe, OpenCV, NumPy and the Tello SDK.
